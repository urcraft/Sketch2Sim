Welcome to Sketch2Sim 🧪

Sketch or describe a 2D simulation — physics, math, chemistry, biology — and an AI builds a live, interactive page for it on the spot. Everything runs in your browser; your API key never leaves this device.

1 · Add an API key

The app calls an AI provider directly from your browser, so you bring your own key. Pick a provider and grab a key (the free tiers are usually enough to get started):

    Then open , choose your provider and model, paste the key, and Save. Each model in the dropdown is labelled (most capable / balanced / cheapest) to help you choose.

    2 · Tips

    • Sketch + describe. Draw your idea in the Sketch panel, tick Attach sketch, and the drawing is sent with your message. Text refines or overrides the sketch.
    • Iterate in the same chat. Follow-ups like “make gravity adjustable” or “add a velocity arrow” modify the current simulation instead of rebuilding it.
    • Pick a visual style next to the composer (Whiteboard, Lab notebook, Playful, Minimal dark, Retro pixel) to keep generated sims visually consistent.
    • Import an image into the sketch (🖼️) to trace or annotate a diagram.
    • Collapse the sketch (▾) to give the simulation more room, and use the ⛶ buttons to go fullscreen.
    • Conversations persist — each keeps its own sketch and history. Switch with the dropdown, start a new one with ＋, or remove one with 🗑️.
    • If a sim errors, a notice with “Ask AI to fix” appears so you can round-trip the error back to the model.

    3 · Try an example

    Click one to open a new chat with the prompt ready to edit. Nothing is sent — tweak the text or add a sketch, then press Send when you’re ready.
